How this works
  • Hours needed each week = hours left ÷ weeks until your deadline.
  • Each client you see counts as about one hour a week. Your finish month assumes your caseload stays about the same, so treat it as a rough guide.
  • Relational hours count toward your total hours.
  • Your numbers are saved on this device only. Nothing is sent anywhere, and there is no account.
